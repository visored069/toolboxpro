Random Number Generator

Generate cryptographically secure random numbers. Choose your range, count, and format.

10

Generate random numbers in any range — integers for games, giveaways, and sampling, or decimals for simulations. Choose how many numbers you need, set min/max bounds, optionally sort the output, and roll dice with the built-in dice mode.

How to generate random numbers

  1. Set your minimum and maximum values.
  2. Choose how many numbers to generate, and integer or decimal mode.
  3. Optionally enable sorting (ascending, descending) or use dice mode.
  4. Click Generate and copy the results.

Fair picks for giveaways

Every value in the range is equally likely. For a giveaway among numbered entries, generate one integer between 1 and the entry count — that’s the whole procedure, and it’s as fair as the underlying randomness.

Range 1–100, count 1, integer mode  →  one uniformly random winner

Frequently asked questions

How random are these numbers?

The tool uses your browser’s random source — cryptographically strong randomness, suitable for games, draws, and sampling. It is not certified for cryptographic key generation or gambling regulation.

Can the same number appear twice?

Yes — draws are independent by default, so duplicates are possible (and expected in large sets). If you need unique values, generate and de-duplicate, or use a range equal to the count.

What’s dice mode?

A quick preset that simulates dice rolls — integers from 1 to the number of sides you pick.
