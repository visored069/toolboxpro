Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

AI Directories ("we", "our", or "us") operates the aidirectories.in website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information, and we operate no user accounts or analytics. The developer tools on this site process your input locally in your browser, and nothing you type into them is transmitted to us. Two tools call third-party APIs by design: IP Lookup retrieves your general network details from ipapi.co (which sees your IP address, as with any web request), and the QR Code Generator sends the text you encode to api.qrserver.com to render the image. The AI tools listed in our directory are external services with their own privacy practices.

3. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We use your browser's localStorage to save preferences on your device — such as your theme, favorites, and recently used tools — and this data never leaves your browser. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

4. Service Providers

The site is served through static hosting and content-delivery networks. Like any website, these providers see standard technical request data (such as your IP address) when your browser loads pages and assets. We do not share personal information with them, because we do not collect any.

5. Security

The Service uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Because our tools process data locally and we maintain no user accounts or databases of personal information, the personal data we hold is minimal by design. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@aidirectories.in
